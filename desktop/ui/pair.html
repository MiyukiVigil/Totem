Pair with your phone

  1. Open Totem on your phone
  2. Settings → Pair with a PC
  3. Scan this code

The code works for 3 minutes.

Check that shows the same number, then tap Codes match on the phone:

If the numbers are different, tap “They don't” on the phone.

Press Ctrl+Shift+A anywhere to get a code. Totem keeps running in the tray.
